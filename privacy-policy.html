ZedPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

ZedPulse is a health and wellness application designed to help users manage health readings, medications, reminders and related wellness information. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, information may include your name and phone number, account details, health readings you enter, medication records and schedules, reminders, activity statistics, and information you submit for diet advice. If you create a doctor-sharing link, information included in that link may also be processed.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to manage accounts and sign-ins, display health readings, organise medication records and reminders, calculate progress statistics, provide requested diet suggestions, enable sharing features, maintain the service and respond to support requests.

3. Health Information and AI Advice

Information submitted for AI-generated advice may be processed by the services used to provide that feature. AI-generated diet or wellness suggestions are informational only and are not a diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be transmitted to and processed by the app's backend and service providers. Reasonable measures are intended to protect information, but no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process information as needed to operate features. If you create a doctor-sharing link, anyone who obtains that link may be able to view the information included on its page. Share links carefully and keep them private.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet applicable operational, security and legal requirements. To request account and associated personal information deletion, email chimude8584@gmail.com with the subject “ZedPulse Account and Data Deletion Request”. Identity verification may be required. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can choose which optional information to enter and whether to use optional features. You may contact us to ask about your information or request correction or deletion. Your rights depend on applicable privacy laws.

8. Children's Privacy

ZedPulse must not be used to collect children's personal information in a way that violates applicable law. Where required, parental consent or another lawful basis must be obtained.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app and its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
